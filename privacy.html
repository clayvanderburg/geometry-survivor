Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Short version: there are no accounts, ads or third-party trackers. Your progress stays in your browser. If you leave the boxes checked, your callsign and score go on the public leaderboard and an anonymous run summary (no name, no IP) helps balance the game. You can turn both off on the title screen.

This policy covers VEXOLITH // SURVIVOR (the "game") and this website, run by Clay Vanderburg ("we", "us").

1. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following in your browser's local storage so it is there next time you play. It never leaves your device unless described below:

  • Progress: banked Void Cores and permanent Armory upgrades.
  • Your pilot callsign and your own run history ("MY RUNS").
  • Settings: volume, game mode, damage numbers, radar, sharing choices, which patch notes you've read, and similar preferences.

Clearing your browser's site data deletes all of it. We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

2. Public global leaderboard

When "Post my runs to the public GLOBAL leaderboard" is checked, finishing a run sends that run's callsign, score, wave, level, kills, multiplier, run time, date, game version and game mode to our server. These appear publicly on the leaderboard for anyone to see. Don't use your real name or anything personal as a callsign.

To limit spam, the server derives a salted, one-way hash from your IP address to rate-limit submissions. Your IP address itself is not stored and is not shown on the leaderboard.

Uncheck the box on the title screen to stop posting. Runs you already posted stay on the board unless you ask us to remove them (see section 6).

3. Anonymous gameplay statistics

When "Share anonymous session stats" is checked, finishing a run sends a summary of that run (for example weapons and upgrades chosen, enemies faced, wave reached, run length, game version and mode) so we can balance the game. It contains no callsign and no IP address; a salted IP hash is used only to rate-limit uploads. Very short runs and runs using developer tuning are not sent.

Turn it off on the title screen or in the pause menu under Settings.

4. Feedback form

If you use the feedback form, we receive what you type, plus your email address if you choose to give it. We use it only to read and reply to your message. Form submissions are processed and stored by our host, Netlify.

5. Hosting and service providers

The site, leaderboard and statistics run on Netlify, which, like any web host, processes technical request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the site and keep it secure. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.

6. Your choices and requests

  • Turn leaderboard posting and stats sharing on or off at any time on the title screen.
  • Delete local progress and settings by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • To have a leaderboard entry removed, or with any privacy question, send a message through the feedback page with your callsign and the approximate date and score. Anonymous statistics can't be traced back to you, so they can't be singled out for deletion.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data about you; contact us to use them.

7. Retention

Leaderboard entries are kept for as long as the leaderboard exists. Anonymous run statistics are kept to compare balance across versions. Rate-limit records hold only a hash and a timestamp. Feedback messages are kept as long as needed to handle them.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. A leaderboard callsign is the only thing a player chooses to publish; if you believe a child has published personal information there, contact us and we'll remove it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
