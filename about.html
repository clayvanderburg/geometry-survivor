About & Credits

A free, browser-based geometric twin-stick roguelite.

VEXOLITH // SURVIVOR is a twin-stick survival game you play in your browser. Pilot a drill-clawed crab ship through a sector of asteroid clusters, carve tunnels, mine gold and Void Cores, and survive ever larger swarms, lieutenants and bosses. It plays with mouse and keyboard, a game controller, or touch, and can be added to your home screen like an app.

It is free to play, has no ads and no accounts, and loads no third-party trackers.

Made by

Designed and developed by Clay Vanderburg, an independent developer. Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome on the feedback page. The 📜 PATCH NOTES button on the title screen lists what changed in each version.

Credits

Music

The soundtrack was generated with Suno by the game's author from prompts written for VEXOLITH, then mastered for the game. If a track can't load, the game falls back to its own procedural synthwave score.

Sound effects

Sound effects were generated for this game with ElevenLabs Sound Effects, then trimmed and normalized. Additional synth sounds are created live in your browser with the Web Audio API.

Inspiration

VEXOLITH draws inspiration from Geometry Wars, Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor and Vampire Survivors. It is an independent fan of those games and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by their developers or publishers.

Tools

Built with plain HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript, hosted on Netlify.
